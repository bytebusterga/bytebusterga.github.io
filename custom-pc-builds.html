How Our Custom PC Build Process Works

01
Tell Me What You Need

Tell me your budget, what you want to use the computer for, and what kind of performance you need. Message me on Facebook or fill out the custom PC build form below.

02
Get a Custom Parts List & Quote

I put together a custom parts list based on your needs and budget, then send you a detailed quote for your approval. There is no commitment until you approve the build.

03
Parts Invoice Is Paid First

Once you approve the custom PC build, the parts cost is invoiced and paid upfront via PayPal before I order anything. No parts are ordered without payment, which protects both you and Byte Buster.

04
PC Assembly & Testing

I professionally assemble your custom computer and thoroughly test it before delivery. Testing includes benchmarks, temperature checks, stability testing, and verification that the system is performing as expected. Labor is invoiced separately after the build is completed and verified.

05
Local Pickup or Nationwide Shipping

Pick up your completed custom PC locally in Eastanollee, Georgia, or have it shipped anywhere in the United States. Any remaining labor balance is confirmed before shipping.

How Custom PC Build Labor Is Priced
I charge a labor range rather than a flat fee because no two custom PC builds are exactly the same. Build complexity, cable management, compatibility troubleshooting, cooling configuration, storage setup, and testing requirements can all affect the amount of time involved. The ranges below are honest estimates based on typical custom computer builds. I'll always confirm the final labor charge before shipping or pickup. You are never billed more than what was agreed to.
