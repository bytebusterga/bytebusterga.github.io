PC & Laptop Repair

A computer that won't turn on can have several causes, including a failed power supply, motherboard, RAM, storage device, or other hardware problem. Power connections and startup issues can also prevent a computer from powering on normally. Byte Buster can diagnose the system to identify the cause before recommending a repair.

A computer that powers on but shows no picture can have several possible causes, including RAM, graphics card, motherboard, power, display cable, or monitor problems. Byte Buster can diagnose the system to determine which component or connection is causing the no-display problem before recommending a repair.

Unexpected shutdowns and restarts can be caused by overheating, power supply problems, faulty RAM, storage issues, Windows or driver problems, or other hardware failures. Byte Buster can diagnose the system to determine whether the cause is hardware, software, or a thermal problem.

Yes. Byte Buster can diagnose gaming PCs experiencing problems such as no display, crashes, overheating, unexpected shutdowns, performance issues, and hardware failures. Diagnostics can help identify problems involving the graphics card, RAM, storage, power supply, cooling system, motherboard, or Windows.

04

Virus & Malware Removal

Yes. Byte Buster provides virus and malware removal for Windows PCs and laptops. I can diagnose the system, remove malicious or unwanted software, and check for additional problems caused by the infection. Virus and malware removal is available for local customers in Northeast Georgia and through nationwide mail-in repair.

Common signs of a virus or malware infection include unusual pop-ups, browser redirects, slow performance, unfamiliar programs, security warnings, changed browser settings, or other behavior that was not present before. A diagnostic can determine whether malware is actually responsible and identify other possible causes.

Normally, virus and malware removal does not require deleting personal files. However, if the infection has severely damaged Windows or a clean Windows installation is necessary, I will discuss the situation with you before performing work that could affect your data. Backing up important files before repair is always recommended.

If malware has significantly damaged Windows, removing the infection may not completely resolve the problem. Depending on the condition of the computer, Byte Buster may recommend Windows repair, additional troubleshooting, data recovery, or a clean Windows installation.
