04

Virus & Malware Removal

Yes. Byte Buster can diagnose and remove viruses, malware, unwanted software and other security threats from Windows computers. The system is checked for additional problems that may have been caused by the infection.

Common warning signs include unusual pop-ups, browser redirects, slow performance, unfamiliar programs, security warnings, unexpected changes to Windows or other behavior that wasn't present before. A diagnostic can determine whether malware is actually responsible.

Virus and malware removal is normally performed without deleting personal files. However, if an infection has severely damaged Windows or the system requires an operating system reinstall, I will discuss the situation with you before proceeding with work that could affect your data.

If malware has caused significant Windows damage, removal alone may not be enough. Depending on the condition of the system, I may recommend Windows repair, additional troubleshooting or a clean Windows installation.
